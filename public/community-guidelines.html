Safety first

Community Guidelines

Keep every conversation respectful, safe, and comfortable. If someone crosses a line, skip the chat and report the behavior.

01

Be Respectful

No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, or attempts to shame another person.

02

Keep It Appropriate

Do not share nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, or anything intended to shock or disturb others.

03

Protect Privacy

Do not ask for private details, reveal someone else's personal information, record calls, or share screenshots without consent.

04

Use Real Consent

If someone seems uncomfortable or asks you to stop, respect that immediately. Skipping is always okay.

05

Report Bad Behavior

Help keep ZyroMeet safer by reporting users who violate these guidelines.
